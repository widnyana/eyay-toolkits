# Exemplars (Gold-Standard Perihbahasa)

Curated, human-approved perihbahasa grouped by the **technique** that makes each one work.
When generating, *imitate these* — they are the quality bar. Each entry lists the **source
proverb**, the **technique**, a **domain tag**, a **mode tag**, and a one-line **why it works**.

Technique names are defined in `../SKILL.md` (Techniques catalog). An exemplar may carry a
secondary technique, noted after `+`.

**Mode tags** (see `../SKILL.md` Anatomy #1):
- `perih` — touches an emotional wound the reader already carries (nyesek/kecewa/campur
  aduk); passes the "literally me" / "kena banget" test. This is the primary target.
- `plesetan` — clever wordplay, technically correct, but doesn't touch a pre-existing ache.
  Fine as secondary output, not the calibration target.

Domain tags: `romance`, `LDR`, `HTS`, `circle`, `keluarga`, `medsos`, `karir`, `office`,
`tech`, `money`, `mudik`, `flirty`, `absurd`.

## Pun-Swap

Swap one key word for a **near-homophone or semantic twin** that flips the meaning while
keeping the original's sound-shape and cadence. The pun must *carry meaning*, not decorate.

- **Habis gelap, terdengarlah erang.**
  *Source:* Habis gelap terbitlah terang. · `romance` · `perih`
  *Why:* `terang → erang` (light → groan) flips Kartini's hope into heartbreak in one
  near-homophone — and the source's cultural weight makes the flip land hard.

- **Sudah jauh tertimpa rindu.**
  *Source:* Sudah jatuh tertimpa tangga. · `LDR` · `perih`
  *Why:* `jatuh → jauh` (a single-vowel shift) maps "compounded bad luck" onto the ache of
  distance: already far apart, *then* hit by longing.

- **rumput sendiri akan lebih hijau ketika dirawat tetangga.**
  *Source:* Rumput tetangga selalu lebih hijau. · `medsos`/`romance` · `perih`
  *Why:* not envy of others' grass — *your own* grass thriving under someone else's care.
  Names the specific ache of infidelity/replacement without saying either word.

- **pekerjaan tetangga selalu terlihat lebih hijau.**
  *Source:* Rumput tetangga selalu lebih hijau. · `karir` · `plesetan`
  *Why:* one noun (`rumput → pekerjaan`) transplants "the grass is greener" onto universal
  job-envy; clever, but doesn't touch a specific pre-existing wound the way the entry above does.

- **Sedia kamu sebelum hujan.**
  *Source:* Sedia payung sebelum hujan. · `flirty` · `plesetan`
  *Why:* `payung → kamu` turns a preparedness proverb into a tender line — sweet rather than
  perih; the pun's a compliment, not a sting.

- **Lempar rindu sembunyi kenangan.**
  *Source:* Lempar batu sembunyi tangan. · `romance` · `perih`
  *Why:* `batu/tangan → rindu/kenangan` transmutes a proverb about *malice* (sneaky harm)
  into one about *love* (secret longing) — same sneaky skeleton, opposite heart.

- **Air susu dibalas dengan air supply.**
  *Source:* Air susu dibalas dengan air tuba. · `romance`/`absurd` · `plesetan`
  *Why:* `tuba → supply` runs on two registers at once — literal ("repaid with mere supply")
  and pop-cultural (the band). The ambiguity is the joke, not a touched wound. +
  *Pun-Swap (double register)*.

- **Kasih ibu sepanjang masa, kasih kamu cuma pas butuh aja.**
  *Source:* Kasih ibu sepanjang masa. · `keluarga`/`romance` · `perih`
  *Why:* keeps the sacred first half intact, then contrasts it with a relationship the reader
  already privately suspects is one-sided — the recognition is the sting, not the line itself.

- **Habis manis, kontak di-block.**
  *Source:* Habis manis sepah dibuang. · `romance`/`medsos` · `perih`
  *Why:* `sepah dibuang → kontak di-block` updates the discard into its exact modern-day
  ritual — ghosting via block, the most literal "sepah" this generation knows.

## Moral-Reversal

Keep the proverb's words or skeleton, but **invert its conclusion** to expose a truth the
original papered over. The comedy is the betrayal of the comfort.

- **Daripada hujan batu di negeri sendiri, lebih baik hujan emas di negeri orang.**
  *Source:* (same proverb, order reversed) · `money` · `plesetan`
  *Why:* same words, swapped halves — patriotism flipped into the brain-drain / cari-duit
  reality. Sharp cynicism, but a stated opinion rather than a touched wound.

- **Pacar teriak tanda terlalu dalam.**
  *Source:* Air beriak tanda tak dalam. · `romance` · `perih`
  *Why:* `beriak → teriak`, then `tak dalam → terlalu dalam` — "loud = shallow" reversed into
  "loud = loves hardest," naming the specific ache of loving someone visibly more. + *Pun-Swap*.

- **Sedikit demi sedikit, lama-lama ditinggal merit.**
  *Source:* Sedikit demi sedikit, lama-lama menjadi bukit. · `romance` · `perih`
  *Why:* accumulation-into-success reversed into accumulation-into-loss (`bukit → merit` keeps
  the `-it` rhyme); small rifts compound until they marry someone else.

- **Semua akan glowing jika ada dananya.**
  *Source:* "Semua akan … jika ada …" (comfort platitude) · `money` · `perih`
  *Why:* a platitude of reassurance injected with the cold truth that money is what actually
  resolves it — the ache of realizing "waktu menyembuhkan" doesn't apply without cash.

## Domain-Transplant

Keep the proverb's logic intact; **relocate the whole thing into a domain with real pain**
(tech, office, mudik, money). The wisdom must apply *exactly* to the new domain.

- **karena async setitik, kena await sebelanga.**
  *Source:* Karena nila setitik, rusak susu sebelanga. · `tech` · `perih`
  *Why:* `nila → async`, `susu → await`. One unhandled await genuinely corrupts a whole
  promise chain — the small-thing-ruins-big-thing wisdom maps *exactly* onto a developer pain.

- **Senior kencing berdiri, junior kencing direview.**
  *Source:* Guru kencing berdiri, murid kencing berlari. · `office`/`karir` · `perih`
  *Why:* `berlari → direview` flips the teacher/student comic escalation into the junior
  grievance: seniors ship freely, juniors get every action micromanaged.

- **Ingin hati pulang tenggo, apa daya kerjaan tak sampai.**
  *Source:* Hasrat hati memeluk gunung, apa daya tangan tak sampai. · `mudik`/`karir` · `perih`
  *Why:* "desire vs ability" mapped onto mudik — the hands can't reach the mountain, the work
  never reaches "done," so you can't go home for Lebaran.

- **Sekali vouching, dua tiga contreng terlampaui.**
  *Source:* Sekali merengkuh dayung, dua tiga pulau terlampaui. · `office`/`medsos` · `plesetan`
  *Why:* one act of endorsement secures multiple approvals/checkmarks — clever mechanic-swap,
  but no pre-existing ache underneath it.

## Punchline-Continuation

Keep the original *intact* as bait 1, then **add a fresh line** that escalates into a specific
gut-punch. The proverb sets the frame; the added line delivers the moment.

- **Air susu dibalas air tuba. Jadian lagi seminggu move-onnya sedasawarsa.**
  *Source:* Air susu dibalas air tuba. · `romance` · `perih`
  *Why:* the continuation carries the real sting — `seminggu : sedasawarsa` is the asymmetric
  math of heartbreak. + *Numeric-Asymmetry*.

- **HARIMAU MATI MENINGGALKAN BELANG. MANTAN PERGI MENINGGALKANMU PAS LAGI SAYANG-SAYANGNYA.**
  *Source:* Harimau mati meninggalkan belang. · `romance` · `perih`
  *Why:* ALL-CAPS is a tonal choice (righteous rage — the one sanctioned break from deadpan);
  the specificity of "pas lagi sayang-sayangnya" (right when you love them most) is the
  near-universal gut-punch. + *Tone*.

## Parallel-Couplet

Rewrite **both halves** into a new parallel pair that mirrors the original's grammatical
balance — or build a fresh `X … Y …` pantun-style couplet on a shared near-homophone.

- **Anak dipangku dilepaskan, beruk di rimba disusui. Pacar di bahu dilepaskan,
  selingkuhan di bahu orang lain disayangi.**
  *Source:* Anak dipangku dilepaskan, beruk di rimba disusui. · `romance` · `perih`
  *Why:* the "caring for the wrong recipient" logic mirrors exactly onto infidelity — yours
  dropped, another's cherished.

- **Niat hati ke penghulu, ditikung teman kemudian.**
  *Source:* Berakit-rakit ke hulu, berenang-renang ke tepian; bersakit-sakit dahulu … ·
  `romance`/`circle` · `perih`
  *Why:* `penghulu` is a double-meaning (continues the `hulu` sound *and* = the wedding
  official); the delayed-gratification moral becomes a betrayal-by-a-friend punchline.

- **mantan pergi meninggalkan kenangan, gebetan pergi menanggalkan harapan.**
  *Source:* "X pergi meninggalkan Y" pantun form. · `romance`/`HTS` · `perih`
  *Why:* `meninggalkan / menanggalkan` (leave-behind vs strip-away) is a gorgeous near-
  homophone that distinguishes two flavors of loss — the defined one and the undefined one.

## Numeric-Asymmetry

Use the original's `se-… se-…` / numeric skeleton to stage a **tiny-cause : huge-effect** or
**huge-investment : tiny-return** contrast. The math is felt, not stated.

- **Putus satu, nunggu seribu.**
  *Source:* "satu … seribu …" template. · `romance` · `perih`
  *Why:* the instantly-recognizable numeric backbone; one breakup → a thousand nights of
  waiting for a text that won't come.

- **Gara-gara 'Hai' sebaris, rusak move-on setahun.**
  *Source:* Karena nila setitik, rusak susu sebelanga. · `romance`/`medsos` · `perih`
  *Why:* same skeleton as the async exemplar, different domain — one line of text wrecks a
  year of healing. `sebaris : setahun`.

- **rindu setahun dihapus jumpa sehari.**
  *Source:* Panas setahun hilang oleh hujan sehari. · `LDR` · `perih`
  *Why:* a year of yearning erased by one day of meeting — the LDR reunion truth. `setahun :
  sehari`.

## Pantun-Lyrical

Prioritize **phonetic beauty and cadence** over punch; build a couplet on alliteration /
assonance. Use when the goal is poetic, not punchy.

- **Malam menyingsing rindu menyongsong.**
  *Source:* pantun lyric form. · `romance` · `plesetan`
  *Why:* `menyingsing / menyongsong` (rising vs welcoming) is near-identical sound, opposite
  motion — pure poetry, prioritizing beauty over sting. Shows the skill can do lyrical, not
  just punchy.
